Breakfast is never hurried — it is the sacred start of the day. 02 Banana Leaf Feasts A proper sadya or virundu is served on a fresh banana leaf — items placed in a precise order, eaten with the right hand, each dish a counterpart to the next. The leaf itself is an ingredient in the experience. 03 Festival Foods Pongal for Pongal, Kozhukattai for Vinayagar Chaturthi, Payasam for every auspicious moment — the calendar of South India is written in sweet and savoury offerings to gods and family alike. 04 The Art of Tempering Tadka — or thalikkal — is not an afterthought. The precise moment of adding mustard, urad dal, dried chillies and asafoetida to shimmering oil transforms every dish, adding depth, texture and fragrance unmistakably South Indian. KNOW YOUR PANTRY Essential Spices of the South 🌶️ Dried Red Chilli காய்ந்த மிளகாய் The backbone of heat. Used whole in tempering, ground for chilli powder. Kashmiri variety gives colour without excessive heat. 🟡 Turmeric மஞ்சள் Sacred and medicinal. Used in almost every savoury preparation. Fresh root used in festive foods; powder in daily cooking. 🌿 Curry Leaves கறிவேப்பிலை The defining aroma of South Indian cooking. Always used fresh. Added to hot oil at the start of any dish — never a garnish. ⚫ Mustard Seeds கடுகு Added to nearly every dish via tadka. Wait for the pop — that is the moment when oil is ready and flavour blooms. 🔴 Tamarind புளி The sour soul of Tamil cooking. Used in rasam, sambar, puliyodharai, and countless others. Buy in block form, soak and extract. 🤍 Asafoetida பெருங்காயம் A pinch is all it takes. Pungent raw, transformative cooked. Essential in dal, rasam, and for those avoiding onion and garlic.
