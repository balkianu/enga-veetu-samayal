Know Your Pantry Essential Spices of the South 🌶️ Dried Red Chilli காய்ந்த மிளகாய் The backbone of heat. Used whole in tempering, ground for chilli powder. Kashmiri variety gives colour without excessive heat. 🟡 Turmeric மஞ்சள் Sacred and medicinal. Used in almost every savoury preparation. Fresh root used in festive foods; powder in daily cooking. 🌿 Curry Leaves கறிவேப்பிலை The defining aroma of South Indian cooking. Always used fresh. Added to hot oil at the start of any dish — never a garnish. ⚫ Mustard Seeds கடுகு Added to nearly every dish via tadka. Wait for the pop — that is the moment when oil is ready and flavour blooms. 🔴 Tamarind புளி The sour soul of Tamil cooking. Used in rasam, sambar, puliyodharai, and countless others. Buy in block form, soak and extract. 🤍 Asafoetida பெருங்காயம் A pinch is all it takes. Pungent raw, transformative cooked. Essential in dal, rasam, and for those avoiding onion and garlic.
